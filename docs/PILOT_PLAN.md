# Controlled Pilot Plan

## Scope

Start with one neighborhood or one agency partner, 25-100 invited residents, and a named representative/moderator team. Do not market the service as citywide until the pilot gates pass.

## Pilot goals

- 80% of invited residents can submit a report without assistance.
- 95% of valid submissions receive a confirmation ID.
- Duplicate suggestions are understandable and actionable.
- Moderators review new reports within one business day.
- No cross-user edit, delete, media, or role-escalation failures.
- All incidents have an owner and response target.

## Weekly review

Review report volume, duplicate rate, moderation queue age, resolution time, abuse attempts, upload failures, accessibility feedback, and representative response quality.

## Exit gates

Expand only after two consecutive weeks without critical authorization incidents, with tested backups, working recovery email, monitoring alerts, a documented moderation policy, and written agency consent for the next geography.
